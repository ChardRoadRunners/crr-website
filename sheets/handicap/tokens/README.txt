CRR finish tokens 1-40

stl/  one file per token, each one solid piece. 50 x 36 x 3.4 mm, ~7 g in PLA.
      Print flat as they come (mark side up), no supports.

  Top: the CRR mark and the number, small, cut 1 mm in.
  Underside: the number, big, flush, outlined by a 0.6 mm groove for colouring in by hand.
  Same settings as the test token 10 that came out well.

To change anything, edit the values at the top of crr-finish-token.scad in OpenSCAD,
keeping mark.scad and Manrope-ExtraBold.ttf alongside. Manrope: SIL Open Font Licence.
