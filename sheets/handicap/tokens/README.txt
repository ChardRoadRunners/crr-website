CRR finish tokens 1-40

stl/          one file per token, ready for the slicer. 50 x 36 x 3.4 mm.
              Front: the number. Back: the CRR mark, with the number small.
              Everything is cut 1 mm into each face. Print flat, front up, no supports.
              Two colours: change filament at 2.4 mm (where the front cut begins),
              e.g. purple body, sky-blue numbers. Or fill the numbers with a paint pen.

To change anything (size, depth, hole, lean of the numbers), edit the numbers at the
top of crr-finish-token.scad in OpenSCAD, keeping mark.scad and Manrope-ExtraBold.ttf
in the same folder. Manrope is under the SIL Open Font Licence (Manrope-OFL.txt).
