Chard Town Council have decided that the planned Santa Run will not go ahead this year due to concerns about H&S and insurance

"Our areas of concerns are primarily that our Safeguarding Policy was deferred at a meeting on Monday night, as Designated Safeguarding Officer training needs to be first completed" 

Tim Bickham

Tim went on to say: "I don't want the work I and others have done to be wasted so I hope we can make a go of it in the future. Thank you for your offer of involvement and sorry to deliver this news"
