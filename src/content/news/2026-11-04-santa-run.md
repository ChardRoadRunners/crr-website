**Advance Notice**

Chard Town Council are organising a Santa Run with three short races for children aged under 5, 5-9 and 10-16.

- This is planned for Saturday 28th November immediately before the Christmas lights are switched on.
- They have requested assistance from Road Runners. Ideally they would like someone, dressed in a Santa outfit (supplied), to lead the runners; two or more tail runners, and as many marshals as we can manage.
- The routes have not yet been finalised but will be restricted to closed roads in the town centre.

I will be asking for volunteers following a Chard Town Council planning meeting on November  6th
