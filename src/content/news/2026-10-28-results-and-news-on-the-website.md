The club website now carries the results as well as the race reports.

- **The monthly handicap**: every night has its own page, with the full result, the quickest run and who beat their start time by the most.
- **The championship**: the standings, race by race, updated every night from the championship sheet.
- **Find your name**: on the Results page, type your name to see your championship standing and every handicap you've run, together.
- **Club news**: the latest results, race reports and club notices are at the top of the homepage, and all of them are on the News page.

Results still come from the same sheets as before, so nothing changes about how you submit a championship result or how the handicap is timed.
