## What personal data we hold

You may give us information about you by filling in forms at an event or
online, or by corresponding with us by phone, email or otherwise. This includes
information you provide when you register with the club, or when you take part
in discussions on social media.

The information you give us may include your name, date of birth, address,
email address, phone number, the England Athletics affiliated clubs you are
registered with, and gender. We may also ask for relevant health information,
which is classed as special category personal data.

We only collect data we need in order to run the club. If we don't need it, we
don't collect it.

## Why we need it

We need this information to administer your membership and provide the services
you sign up to when you join. Our lawful basis for processing it is that we have
a contractual obligation to you as a member.

Reasons we process your data include:

- sharing personal data with club coaches or leaders to run training sessions
- sharing personal data with club team managers to enter events
- sharing personal data with leagues, county associations and other competition
  providers for event entry
- sharing personal data with governing bodies such as England Athletics and the
  Association of Running Clubs
- sharing personal data with Connect My Club, which the club uses to administer
  membership

## This website

This website is a set of static pages. It does not set cookies, does not track
visitors, and has no analytics.

## Photographs

Race reports and club news often include photos from races and club nights,
shared by the members who took them. We only publish a photo when the people
in it are happy for it to be shared.

If you are in a photo on this website and would rather not be, tell us through
the [contact page](/contact) and we will take it down. You don't need to give a
reason.

We don't publish a directory of members' photos and names. Anything like that
would only happen with each person's own consent.
