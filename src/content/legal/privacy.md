## What personal data we hold

You may give us information about you by filling in forms at an event or
online, or by corresponding with us by phone, email or otherwise. This includes
information you provide when you register with the club, or when you take part
in discussions on social media.

The information you give us may include your name, date of birth, address,
email address, phone number, the England Athletics affiliated clubs you are
registered with, and gender. We may also ask for relevant health information,
which is classed as special category personal data.

We only collect data we need in order to run the club. If we don't need it, we
don't collect it.

## Why we need it

We need this information to administer your membership and provide the services
you sign up to when you join. Our lawful basis for processing it is that we have
a contractual obligation to you as a member.

You don't have to share your personal data with the club. But if you choose not
to, we may not be able to register you or administer your membership.

Reasons we process your data include:

- processing membership forms and payments
- sharing personal data with club coaches or leaders to run training sessions
- sharing personal data with club team managers to enter events
- sharing personal data with leagues, county associations and other competition
  providers for event entry
- sharing personal data with governing bodies such as England Athletics and the
  Association of Running Clubs
- sharing personal data with Connect My Club, which the club uses to administer
  membership
- telling you about club activities, membership renewals and social events,
  mostly through the club WhatsApp community and sometimes by email
- publishing race and competition results

## Results

We publish results on this website, including the club championship and the
monthly handicap. A published result shows the runner's name and time.

If you run a handicap as a guest, your name and time are kept in the club's
timing records and published with the results, the same as a member's. If you
would rather your name was not published, email us (see
[Who to contact](#who-to-contact)).

## Social media

The club has a Facebook page, an Instagram account, a club on Strava and a
WhatsApp community. Members are free to join them, and anyone can join the
Strava club. Anyone taking part on a club channel is expected to follow the
club's [social media policy](/social-media).

Each of these platforms has its own privacy policy, and the club is not
responsible for those. Please check them before you share personal data on the
club's pages.

## Who we share it with

When you join the club you can also choose to register as a member of England
Athletics Limited (company number 05583713). We encourage it, and you will need
to be registered with England Athletics if you want to compete. If you choose
to register, we give England Athletics your personal data so they can set up
your MyAthletics portal account, where you can set and change your own privacy
settings. If you don't choose to register, we don't share your data with
England Athletics. Questions about how England Athletics uses your data go to
[dataprotection@englandathletics.org](mailto:dataprotection@englandathletics.org).

We may also share your personal data with other relevant third parties,
including relevant regulators and authorities such as the Information
Commissioner's Office and the police.

## Transfers outside the UK

Some of the services the club uses to store and process data, such as Google
(Drive, Sheets and Forms), Connect My Club and Cloudflare (email forwarding),
may store it outside the UK. Where that happens, it is with appropriate
safeguards in place to keep your data secure.

## Criminal records and automated decisions

The club does not collect criminal convictions data. No automated
decision-making or profiling takes place.

## This website

This website is a set of static pages. It does not set cookies, does not track
visitors, and has no analytics.

## Photographs

Race reports and club news often include photos from races and club nights,
shared by the members who took them. When you join the club you agree to
appearing in these photos, unless you tick the box on the membership form to
say you'd rather not.

If you are in a photo on this website and would rather not be, tell us and we
will take it down (see [Who to contact](#who-to-contact)). You don't need to
give a reason.

We don't publish a directory of members' photos and names. Anything like that
would only happen with each person's own consent.

## How long we keep it

We keep your personal data for as long as you are a member, and for one year
after your membership ends. After that we delete it, unless a claim has been
brought or is expected. Your details are updated every year when you renew. We
don't use your data for anything other than what this page describes, or what
you have otherwise agreed to.

## Your rights

You may have the right to be told how your personal data is collected and used;
to ask at any time for a copy of it, or for it to be corrected or deleted; to
restrict or object to some kinds of processing, including direct marketing; to
have your data transferred to you or someone else; and, where we rely on your
consent, to withdraw that consent at any time.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office,
the UK's data protection regulator, at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk). We'd
appreciate the chance to put things right first, so please contact us below.

## Who to contact

To ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to have a photo
taken down, or with any question about this page, email our membership
secretary at
[membership@chardroadrunners.com](mailto:membership@chardroadrunners.com). We
will reply within one month.
