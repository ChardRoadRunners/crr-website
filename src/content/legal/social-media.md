## Who it covers

This policy applies to members, committee members, coaches and run leaders,
and anyone else who joins a club group or page. It also covers posts elsewhere
that name the club or its members.

## The club's channels

These are the club's official channels. Only these speak for the club. Other
groups set up by members are welcome, but are not club channels.

| Channel | What it's for | Who can join |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp community | The main way the club talks to members: runs, races, socials, notices | Members |
| [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100057113633601) | Public news, race promotion, photos | Anyone |
| [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/chard_road_runners) | Public photos and race promotion | Anyone |
| [Strava club](https://www.strava.com/clubs/246805/leaderboard) | Sharing runs and routes | Anyone |
| This website | Race reports, results, club news | Anyone can read it; club webmasters post |

## What we expect on club channels

Treat people online as you would at a club run. In practice:

- Be kind and encouraging, whatever someone's pace or experience.
- No bullying, harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in public posts
  or private messages.
- Keep disagreements polite. If it can't be, take it to a committee member
  rather than the group.
- Don't post anything offensive, explicit or illegal, or share other people's
  content without the right to.
- Keep it on topic. The WhatsApp community is busy, so post things in the
  group they belong in.
- Join the groups that are relevant to you.
- Please don't mute the Announcements channel. It's the best place you'll get
  genuinely important information.
- Used kit can be sold in the group, but keep the haggling to a private chat.
  For any other selling or advertising, ask an admin first.
- Don't speak for the club unless the committee has asked you to.
- Criticism of a race, an event or another club stays constructive, and never
  names or shames individuals.

Admins can remove posts that break these rules, and remove someone from a group
if needed.

## Photos, names and personal data

This section follows the club's [privacy page](/privacy).

- Members agree to appear in race and club photos when they join, unless they
  opt out on the membership form. Check before posting a close-up of someone
  who may have opted out.
- Take a photo down promptly if anyone in it asks. They don't have to give a
  reason.
- Never post a member's phone number, address, date of birth, or health or
  injury details, or share them from a club group.
- Don't post photos that pair members with their names as a who's who without
  each person's consent.
- Results and race reports can name runners. That is already public on this
  website.
- In the WhatsApp groups, other members can usually see your phone number.
  Anyone who doesn't want that can follow club news on Facebook, Instagram or
  this website instead.

## Young people and safeguarding

The club's [welfare page](/welfare) and safeguarding policy apply online too.

- Club membership starts at 16, so some members are 16 or 17. Adults don't
  message them privately. Contact goes through a parent or carer, or a group
  that includes one.
- Photos of under-18s need a parent or carer's consent, and are never posted
  with the young person's full name.
- Anything online that worries you about someone's welfare goes to the welfare
  officer at
  [welfare@chardroadrunners.com](mailto:welfare@chardroadrunners.com), not into
  a group chat.

## Concerns and breaches

If you see something on a club channel that breaks this policy, tell an admin
of that channel or any committee member. A welfare concern goes to the welfare
officer.

The committee will usually start with a quiet word. Serious or repeated
breaches are handled under the disciplinary process in the club's
[rules and constitution](/rules-and-constitution), and can lead to removal from
club groups or, in the end, from the club. Anything that may be a crime is
passed to the police.
