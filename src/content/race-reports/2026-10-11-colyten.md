Today at ColyTEN racing was Tracey Ellis-Trott, Mike Egan, Nick Gray and myself. 

Very technical, tricky terrain with a lot of non-runnable hills. Had to keep vigilant as it would be very easy to trip and fall. 

All CRRs did excellent times! I managed 2 hours 5 minutes which I am super proud of considering my target time was under 2 and a half hours.
