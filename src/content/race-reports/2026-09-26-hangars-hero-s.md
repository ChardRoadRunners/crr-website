3rd week, 3rd marathon.  Due to lack of marshals the 26.2 mile loop had been changed to 2 laps of the half marathon out and back course. There was an option, to change to the half or get a refund but the event is for charity so we stuck with it. The field was very small but Chard was well represented by myself and Paul Masters making up almost a quarter of the marathon finishers. The race starts from Bagwell farm caravan park , heads towards the coast and for the majority of the race there's a good view of Chesil beach and nature reserve. It goes past Moonfleet manor. I don't know if anyone else had yo read the book for English comprehension,  all about smuggling and navy press gangs, as far as I can remember. It heads up a female dog of a hill to St Catherine's,  down the other side to rejoin the path you had arrived on. For some unknown reason out and backs are more enjoyable if there's a loop at the end rather than about face and back the exact same way. 

At least from mile 7 you knew exactly what you were in for and it's quite nice cheering on other runners as the head in the opposite direction. This week's race had 2949 ft of elevation but I finished in 6:13:27, just about pulling a negative split by about 30 seconds as we hit the halfway turn around at 3 hours 7 minutes mainly due to having Sally close behind me, knowing she was stronger on the hills than I was and I fancied being 2nd lady. There were only 3 of us but I'm taking it. 

My photos are pants so I've borrowed one from the lovely Emma Challis who deservedly came first lady, possibly because of the inspirational company of Paul on the first out and back.
