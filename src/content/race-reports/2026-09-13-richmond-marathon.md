I'm a bit behind but here's the report on Richmond marathon.  

It was supposed to be my A race, which I was going to do some proper training for, intervals, hills, the lot but the summer hit and I discovered running in over 30 ° is not my thing. The race came along and my chances of getting sub 4  hours were long gone, no deferrals or transfers so I just had to turn up and enjoy it.  

The 1st 6ish miles were twisting and turning along the paths of Kew gardens. (Up to 4 people  can come in for free with the runner). Then we headed out upstream along  the banks of the Thames, turning around at Hampton Court Palace and heading back to the old deer park at Richmond.  The course was not as much tarmac as I expected with a lot of gravel paths but with a number of pacers and a waved start a fast time is possible. 

 It's just within the qualifying period for the following year's London marathon. There are some lovely beer and breakfasts to be had in Brentford. The medal was sparkly and spinny, the times shirt had long sleeves and I definately  wouldn't rule out doing it again.
