We seemed to have a lot less people for this year's race and it appeared to be a couples thing plus me. With Chris and Maria Wadey, Jen and Wayne Loveridge and Helen Hickley attending. The main appeal is the traditional village fete that goes along with it, dog show, veg and craft competition along with various stalls and classic cars. The sausage rolls come highly recommended.

The course itself is mainly up with around a mile of down at the end. It's an unusual distance, being 5 miles not a 10k bit there must be something about it as we keep going back.

One thing I have learnt is that after 14 years of running I still can't manage a proper recovery run as I finished faster than last year.
