The highlight of this half marathon is how it meanders through the four central London parks – Hyde Park, Green Park, St James’ Park and Kensington Gardens, passing Big Ben, Buckingham Palace, the Albert Hall, and you get to run up and down the Mall – so many iconic city sites. Even though there were 17000 runners it was extremely well organised, on par with London marathon. 

Support was amazing especially as it was easy for them to see you in multiple locations. I started determined to enjoy it with the expectation of getting around in about 3 hours. However once I started I was feeling good, comfortable at a pace I normally can only hold for a short time. Not sure if it was the great twisty course, being carried along by the crowds and runners, and great conditions but it stuck with me throughout. So I was really surprised and pleased to finish in the time I did – 2hrs 16m. This race has restored my running mojo/confidence. Anyone contemplating doing a half, I would highly recommend this one – I think the most enjoyable one I’ve done.
