As we all know AGMs can be a bit dull so the 100 marathon club tempt its members to attend by offering a chance to run a marathon.  It's not compulsory,  being a timed event, a soft 6 hours, you can stop at 5k if you like and you don't have to be a club member to enter. This year's race was in Newbury and not liking early starts, I found a national trust car park, (one that wasn't an infamous dogging spot) and car camped, ready for the race the following day.The course was slightly hillier than I had anticipated and seemed to be mainly down on the way out and mainly up on the way back, although there was one section which was up in both directions.  It was a pretty course, with a loop around a lake before heading back. Having 1762 ft of elevation it took a little longer than the previous week's marathon and I finished in 6:18:09.
