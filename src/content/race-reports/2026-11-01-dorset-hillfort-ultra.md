A loop from Maiden Newton with around 1,400 feet of elevation. Taking in Eggardon Hill, rolling hills & woodlands. There would have been views of Lyme bay & Portland Bill but unfortunately we had mist for most of the day. This is a self Navigation route & you do need to have your wits about you so not to take wrong turn or end up the wrong side of the hedge. Took it nice & steady & buddied up with Emma Challis . She saved me from the cows. We came across the line in 8hrs 48 I believe. Here’s a few pics of hers I stole.
