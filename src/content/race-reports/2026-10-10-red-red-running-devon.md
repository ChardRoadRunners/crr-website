RED ( running events Devon) 

After missing Clarendon last week for a number of reasons today was necessary to keep some miles going. Helen & Chris seem to enjoy these events . If you like out & backs , it’s great. I absolutely hate them! 

The good thing about this  course is that it’s pretty cheap, very friendly , you can do as few or as many miles as you can manage in the time & you can see the sea. 

My foot seems to be holding up well atm . Unfortunately today my right hamstring didn’t like the route either so there was a lot of walking in the second half. However I managed 4.43 & was home just after lunchtime. Face says it all 😏
