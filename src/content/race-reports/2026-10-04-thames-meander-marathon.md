I needed to fit in a Marathon for a bonus Championship point so I searched for flat races and found the Thames Meander organised by Hermes Running. 

It was a well organised event with half marathon and 10k events on the same day with starts staggered by 30minutes each. This meant I passed 7 aid stations because it was an out and back  half marathon which I completed twice. It was a bit misty by the river for the first hour but it soon burt off and lovely sunshine shone through for the rest of the morning. 

As we got closer to midday the tow path got busier with dog walkers, casual runners and people just out enjoying the sunshine, the whole event had a really vibe vibe to it and I recomend it. The path itself was a mixture of tarmac and rough gravel surface. One small part was covered in slippery silt which was deposited by the Thames when the tide came in, but once encountered it was easy to take it a bit steady.

 Manager to finish in 4hhours 39minutes, I ran for 4min 30sec and walked for 30 sec the whole time using the system recomended by Geoff Galaway, the American Olympian. I found the 30seconds walk great for recovery and my heart rate only climbed by about 6bpm the whole race, it made the whole thing very enjoyable.
