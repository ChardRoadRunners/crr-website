This was another event held by Running Events Devon on the Granite way near Oakhampton. 

I wanted to do this partly because I like the route, even though it's out and back but mainly because the medal is fab. We met up for dinner in Oakhampton the night before for a catch up and then I spent a chilly night at the Fox and Hounds campsite. 

Turning up and settling off whenever you want I made it to the start line around 8.09 and set off with Baz a fellow 100 marathon club member who was using the jeffing method and running for 1 minute, walking for 30 seconds. Not having successfully used this method before, he graciously let me tag along to his beeps, even taking longer breaks at the snack shack to accommodate my fueling, taking longer at one stop, it took nearly a whole lap to catch up. 

The stop start caused some issues with hip pain and I realised I'd left my caffeine at home. It was a cool start by by the end of the first lap I'd discarded two jackets, later my fleecy arm sleeves and hat came off due to warming up. During lap 5 I began to experience chaffing under the arms, the obvious solution was to discard the vest, although JC was disappointed I stopped there.  Baz continued to drag me along to my fastest marathon since Newport last year in an unofficial time of 5:03. Lessons learnt,  I don't need as much fuel as I usually take, structured run walk is faster than going by feel. Don't forget to lube up at the start.

Chris Wadey also joined the race shortly after, finishing in 5:16.
