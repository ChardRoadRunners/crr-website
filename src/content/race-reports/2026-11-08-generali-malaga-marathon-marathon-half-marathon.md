After the success of last year's autumn marathon season, The Captain said we had to do another one this year, so we set out to find the fastest and most interesting one we could. Would you believe it just happened that the best option available required a 5 day trip to the Costa Del Sol🤥.

Once we had set on the destination, it was surprisingly easy to persuade a few others into joining us. Then we found out there was also a half marathon and roped in a few more.

Fast forward through 4 months of solid training for all involved and we arrived in Málaga 10 strong (including 5 day honorary member Budge).

The 8:30am start was unusually welcome, in order to get round before the afternoon siesta, and the joint marathon and half start meant an opportunity for pacing and support offers across 5 marathon and 5 half marathon runners.

Wayne and Steve off first in the faster waves, while Phil, Jack and Dave followed not far behind. Neil, Matt and Joe stuck together shortly after, whilst Rhys and Budge selected their own start pens.

A fast, flat course with great weather and scenery, there were plenty of opportunities to spot each other out on the course, we also bumped into a few Somerset runners from Stride and Running Forever!

The course highlight was almost certainly running round a track in a stadium 22 miles in, although it was immediately followed by the pain cave that came with the final 4 miles on a dead straight road in the late morning sun.

After the race there was a fantastic opportunity for recovery nutrition and hydration (for 3 days), and we even squeezed in the usual Tuesday night club run while we were there, although this time it was 3 miles no hills for some reason😂.
