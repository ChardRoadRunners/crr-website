Renamed. Exmouth Harriers moved it from Bicton Gardens to Budleigh Salterton Cricket Club, and the organiser describes it as about 10 miles off road, with a 5-mile Blister Lite alongside. 9am start. The 2025 running was 23 November and 2026 was 22 November, both the fourth Sunday. The 2027 date is not published yet.
